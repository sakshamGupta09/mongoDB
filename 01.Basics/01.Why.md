# Why MongoDB

- When should we choose MongoDB over relational databases.


## 1. MongoDB shines when your data is naturally a document

- In relational DBs, we split our data across multiple tables and then join them to get a final version of the output.
- MongoDB lets us store the entire output as one unit. This entire thing is one product. Store and retrieve it as one unit.
