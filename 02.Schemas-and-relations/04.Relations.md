# Relations

- Documents in different collections can be related to each other. How do we map those relations?
- We have two ways of storing relations - Embedded documents and Reference.

## One to One Relations

- User and Passport. One User has exactly one Passport. One Passport belongs to exactly one User.

## Embedding

- We have a strong one to one relationship.
- We are always fetching users with their passports. Passports are never fetched seperately.
