# Indexes

- We have a collection with 100 million documents.
- When we do a read query, MongoDB goes does a collection scan. It goes to every document one by one and sees if it fulfils the criteria.
- This can take considerable amount of time especially if the result set contains only 10 documents. We scanned 100 million documents just to get 10 back.

## Why

- To speed up read, update and delete queries.

## What

- Indexes 
- Without index
